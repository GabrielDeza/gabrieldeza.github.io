\cvsection{Summary}
PhD student in Industrial Engineering at Tel Aviv University working with Tal Raviv and Michal Tzur. Previously completed an MSc in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at the University of California, Berkeley with Candace Yano and a BASc in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto, including research with Nicolas Papernot and the CleverHans Lab. Current research focuses on optimization problems for transportation systems, especially line planning, timetabling, and demand routing in highly connected networks, as well as exact optimization methods spanning multiple stages of the network design problem.
