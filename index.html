Hello! I am a PhD student in Industrial Engineering at Tel Aviv University working with Tal Raviv and Michal Tzur. I previously did my MSc at the University of California, Berkeley in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research working with Candace Yano. During my undergrad in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto, I worked on deep learning and privacy at the CleverHans Lab with Nicolas Papernot. My current research focuses on optimization problems for transportation, specifically line planning, timetabling and demand routing in highly connected networks. More generally, I am interested in exact optimization methods for problems spanning several stages of the network design problem.
